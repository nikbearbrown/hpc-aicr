# PROMPTS — Requesting Access to Explorer

No generation prompts. Nothing in this film is generated by a paid or image-model service: the drawings are Manim, the form card is Remotion, the terminals are the real commands, the dashboard is the training's own screenshot, and the portraits are the Professor Bear set. The one prompt in the film is the viewer's Your Turn prompt (BHTF), which is on screen, read aloud and written in `make_sheet.py` (`YT_PROMPT`).
