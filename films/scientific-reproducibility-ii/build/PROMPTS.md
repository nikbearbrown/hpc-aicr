# PROMPTS

No generation prompts. Nothing in this film uses image, video, audio or text generation services; narration is local Kokoro (`am_onyx`), pictures are Manim and Remotion, terminal text is read from the source or from recorded runs in `captures/`.

The one viewer prompt (BHTF, "Your turn") is for the viewer to paste into Claude; it is shown and read in full, and is adapted from the closing exercise of the written guide (read-only first; two checks).
