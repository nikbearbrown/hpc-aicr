# Optimizing GPU Usage on Explorer

**What this is.** A film in the series *Research Computing on Explorer and AICR* (not yet numbered). **Status:** published.

**Watch the film:** https://youtu.be/CtClyMOR-vI (unlisted).

**Read the guide:** [`guide.md`](guide.md) is our written version of the lesson. It follows the film's structure, adds the exact commands the film cannot carry, and records what we tested ourselves and where the original is wrong or silent.

## Where it comes from

Based on Northeastern University Research Computing's training lesson, presented by Arsalan Akhter: https://github.com/northeastern-rc-training/Optimizing-GPU-Usage-on-Explorer. **This repository does not reproduce that lesson.** The guide and the film are our own rewrite: the structure follows our film, the wording, examples and exercises are ours, and we added what we learned using it. The original remains the authority and belongs to its authors.

## What is in this folder

- `guide.md`: the written lesson (our own words), with the film link, the citation, exercises, and corrections.
- `images/`: our diagrams, each as SVG (editable source) and PNG (embedded in the guide).
